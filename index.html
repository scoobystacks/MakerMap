    About Maker Map

    Maker Map is a community directory of makerspaces, fab labs, library maker labs, and shared shops. Pins are color-coded by how you can get in:

    • Free & public: open at no cost, often at public libraries.
    • Paid access: open to anyone through memberships, day passes, punch cards, or hourly rental.
    • Members only: limited to a university, company, or community group.

    Hours, prices, and equipment change. Always check the space's own site before you go. Each entry notes when it was last verified.

    Your location never leaves your device. It is only used to center the map and sort by distance.

    Built by Jeffrey Blitt. The code is MIT licensed and the data is released under the Open Database License, so anyone can reuse it as long as improvements are shared back. Map tiles by OpenStreetMap contributors.
